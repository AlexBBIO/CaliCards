FAQ

How does checkout work?

Add cards to your cart and check out through Shopify's secure, hosted checkout. Payment and order data are handled entirely by Shopify.

How are cards shipped?

Raw cards: penny sleeve + toploader + team bag in a bubble mailer. Graded slabs: snug-fit case inside a rigid box. Tracking on every order.

Is this a real store yet?

The site is ready to go. When you connect a Shopify store (see js/config.js), the demo cards are replaced by your real inventory and live checkout turns on.
